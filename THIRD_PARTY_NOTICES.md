## dvklab (Hacktemplates)

Four effects on this site are rebuilt from techniques in dvklab's
[Hacktemplates](https://github.com/diiviikk5/Hacktemplates) heroes, adapted to
this site's palette, states and runtime:

| This site | dvklab hero |
| --- | --- |
| `public/assets/js/face.js`: the demo's face made of live code | Signal |
| `public/assets/js/void.js`: the black hole behind contact | Event Horizon |
| `public/assets/js/ember.js`: fire through fluted glass in the wordmark | Furnace |
| `public/assets/js/ascii.js`: the live-character backdrop | the ASCII heroes |
