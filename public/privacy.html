Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version

This site doesn't track you. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels and no third party scripts. Fonts and code are served from this domain.

Hosting

The site runs on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps standard request logs (IP address, browser, time) to run and protect the service, under its own privacy policy.

The live jailbreak demo

When you send a probe, your text goes to this site's /api/probe function. If the live model is switched on, the function forwards that text to Anthropic's API to generate the reply, and Anthropic's privacy policy applies to it. If it isn't, a scripted agent answers inside your browser and your text never leaves your device.

The function doesn't store or log what you send or what comes back. To limit abuse it keeps a short lived count of requests per IP address in memory, which disappears when the function instance shuts down.

Treat it like any public demo: don't paste anything you wouldn't want a model to read.

Stored in your browser

Your theme and accent colour choices (local storage), and whether you've already seen the intro this session (session storage). They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Email

If you email me, I use your address to reply and for nothing else.
